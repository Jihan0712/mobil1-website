Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Allied Lubrication Supply Corp. ("Allied Lubrication," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use this website as an authorized Mobil distributor in the Philippines. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to share with us directly:

Contact form details — your name, email address, enquiry category, and any product or vehicle information you include when submitting an enquiry.

Driver Profile responses — the vehicle type, driving conditions, and usage answers you provide while using the Driver Profile tool, used to generate the recommendation shown on screen.

Search terms — any product, vehicle, or keyword information you enter using the Search feature.

We do not ask for payment details, government IDs, or other sensitive personal information through this site.

How we use information

We use the information collected to respond to enquiries submitted through the Contact page, generate your Driver Profile recommendation, and improve the accuracy and usefulness of our product and engine-care content. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information sharing

We do not share the information you submit with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers who help us operate this website, or where required by law.

This site links out to Shopee and Lazada for purchases. Those marketplaces have their own privacy policies, and any information you provide there is governed by their terms, not ours.

Data retention

We retain enquiry and contact information only for as long as needed to respond to your request and maintain reasonable business records, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted to us by reaching out through the Contact page. Driver Profile answers are not stored beyond your browser session unless you submit them through the Contact form.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The effective date at the top of this section indicates when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the enquiry form on our Contact page.

Accessibility Statement

Effective September 1, 2026

Allied Lubrication Supply Corp. is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who rely on assistive technology.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as a practical target across page structure, color contrast, and keyboard navigation.

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As pages and tools such as Driver Profile and Search are updated, we review them against these standards and make improvements where needed.

Known limitations

Some interactive elements, such as embedded maps or third-party marketplace widgets, are outside our direct control and may not fully meet the same standards. We continue to look for ways to improve or supplement these areas.
